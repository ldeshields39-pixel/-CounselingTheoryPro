# Counseling Theory Pro reading guide

The five decks contain 205 original study prompts, with 41 cards per theory. The case examples are fictional. Terms include named theoretical concepts and plain-language descriptions of counseling responses. Historical claims about mental processes are presented within their theoretical models. These materials support coursework and do not constitute an official examination bank or a clinical decision tool.

## Exam and diagnostic reference

The National Board for Certified Counselors maintains the [National Counselor Examination page and content outlines](https://www.nbcc.org/exams/nce/). Select the outline effective on the scheduled exam date. The American Psychiatric Association maintains [DSM educational resources](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/dsm/educational-resources/) and [updates to DSM-5-TR](https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/dsm/updates-to-dsm/updates-to-dsm-5-tr-criteria-text/).
