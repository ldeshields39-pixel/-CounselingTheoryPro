# Counseling Theory Pro

## App Store description draft

Prepare for counseling classes and exam review in short, focused study sessions. Explore five theory decks, practice recalling a concept before revealing its explanation, and save the cards you want to see again. Follow a weekly plan, test yourself with mixed quizzes, and review your progress by theory.

Work through original counseling vignettes, practice distinguishing plausible responses, and find official NCE and DSM-5-TR study resources in one place.

## Study-data privacy summary

The local native build stores saved cards, review history, and quiz results in the app’s storage on the device. It has no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. Removing the local app can remove that history.

The optional Cloud build stores a Sign in with Apple identifier in local preferences when you sign in. The app uses the device’s iCloud account to synchronize study data with Apple’s key-value storage service. Signing out of the app clears the locally stored app sign-in identifier. The device’s iCloud settings govern iCloud access.

The web app stores study activity in browser storage and caches app files for offline use. Its hosting provider handles requests and access to the hosted site. Links to professional organizations open external services with their own privacy practices. The app requests no client records or case notes.

Before publication, the publisher should provide its support and privacy contact and review the distribution build and hosting practices against its published privacy policy.
